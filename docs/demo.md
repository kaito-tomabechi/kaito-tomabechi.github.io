# Opening videos

The portfolio opens with three videos:

1. **[3D concept · 97 s, with sound](../assets/moke-concept-v7.mp4).** Shows apparatus motion, algorithmic measurement, AI orchestration and an illustrative unattended software repair, then two other use cases of the same AI layer: circuit design and test, and high-frequency electrical measurement. Its sequence and code are conceptual. An actual repair is documented in the [evidence](evidence.md#recovery).
2. **[Real operation · 57 s, with music](../assets/real-operation.mp4)**, with [English captions](../assets/real-operation-en.vtt). A screen recording of the real system: two example devices from one run found, focused and imaged by software, including MOKE images, then one archived autofocus run, an illustration of field steps and the results. Shown at ×3–×8, with most of each acquisition cut.
3. **[Automated CAD output · 26 s, with music](../assets/autocad-automation-momentum-v7.mp4).** A real AutoCAD screen capture of devices and electrodes generated across 60 cells, using a demonstration layout and edited playback speed.

See the [media provenance](media.md#opening-videos).
